Treatments

Treatments designed around your smile

Every treatment begins with a digital scan and a written plan, so you know the result, the timeline and the investment before you commit.

  • Porcelain Veneers

    Hand-layered porcelain corrects chips, gaps, staining and uneven edges in as little as two visits. You preview your new smile as a removable mock-up before a single tooth is prepared.

    • Removable trial smile before prep
    • Hand-layered porcelain, natural translucency
    • Typically completed in 2–3 visits
    Duration
    2–3 visits over 2–3 weeks
    Starting investment
    From $1,450 per tooth
    Book this visit: Porcelain Veneers

  • Dental Implants

    A titanium implant and custom porcelain crown replace a missing tooth so it looks, feels and chews like your own. Guided surgery planned from a 3D scan keeps placement precise and recovery short.

    • 3D-guided implant placement
    • Custom abutment and porcelain crown
    • Comfort sedation available
    Duration
    60–90 min placement · 3–6 months to final crown
    Starting investment
    From $3,800 per tooth
    Book this visit: Dental Implants

  • Clear Aligner Therapy

    Nearly invisible, removable aligners straighten crowded or spaced teeth without brackets or wires. Virtual progress check-ins mean fewer trips to the office.

    • Nearly invisible, removable trays
    • Virtual progress check-ins
    • Fits busy weekday and Saturday schedules
    Duration
    6–18 months · check-ins every 8–10 weeks
    Starting investment
    From $3,900
    Book this visit: Clear Aligner Therapy

  • Professional Teeth Whitening

    In-office whitening lifts years of coffee, tea and wine staining in a single visit, with desensitizing care built in. Custom take-home trays keep your result bright for the long term.

    • In-office lift in one 90-minute visit
    • Desensitizing care included
    • Custom take-home trays for maintenance
    Duration
    90-minute visit
    Starting investment
    From $495
    Book this visit: Professional Teeth Whitening
