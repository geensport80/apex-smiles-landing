Got questions?

Questions patients ask before their first visit

Still unsure? Call (555) 010-2740 and a team member will talk it through with you.

How much will my treatment cost, and can I pay over time?

After your consultation you receive a written, itemized estimate, so the price you agree to is the price you pay. Most treatments can be spread over 6 to 24 months, including 0% financing for 12 months (subject to approval), and members save 15% on every treatment.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. We work with most PPO plans and file every claim for you. Our team verifies your benefits before your first visit and shows exactly what your plan covers on your estimate. Cosmetic treatments are rarely covered by insurance, so we offer financing and our membership plan for those.

I'm nervous about pain. What do you do to keep me comfortable?

You're far from alone, and nothing happens without your go-ahead. We use numbing gel before every injection, take things at your pace, and offer nitrous oxide or oral sedation for longer or more involved visits. Many patients tell us they felt more relaxed than they expected, and you can raise a hand to pause at any time.

Can I book appointments outside normal working hours?

Yes. We open at 7 am on weekdays, stay open until 7 pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and see patients on two Saturdays each month. Many follow-up visits can be done virtually, and you can reschedule with 48 hours' notice at no charge.

What happens at my first consultation?

Your complimentary 45-minute visit begins with a conversation about your goals. We then take photos and a quick 3D scan (no messy impressions), and Dr. Laurent walks you through your options, timeline and investment. You leave with a written plan and no obligation to book.
